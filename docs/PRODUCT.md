# Product direction

ENGAWA is not an office replica and not a 3D skin for chat.

The core object is the **workbench**: a place where purpose, unfinished work, questions, decisions and next actions survive even when the people change.

## Design principles

### A person belongs to more than one place
The primary subject is not one company's virtual building. One person may participate in multiple companies, projects and communities. Identity can travel; organization data cannot leak with it.

### Presence without obligation
`available`, `knock`, `focus`, and `away` are communication choices, not work scores. ENGAWA should make it possible to be present without being interruptible.

### Spatial design is a social protocol
Distance can communicate context, but realism is not the goal. Once a conversation starts, intelligibility should beat physically accurate attenuation. Users need a non-spatial/list alternative.

### Work should be resumable
A late participant should not need to read an entire chat log. The workbench distinguishes confirmed items from unresolved ones and exposes a bounded handoff packet.

### AI listens selectively, not absolutely
Future AI may help summarize or continue work only inside an explicitly allowed scope. A human-authored statement, an AI suggestion, a confirmed decision and permission to execute are different states.

## First measurable hypothesis
Two people can work near each other, briefly coordinate without forcing a meeting, leave one useful decision, and allow a third participant to resume later with less explanation.

Measure separately:
- did spatial presence increase useful lightweight coordination?
- did it increase unwanted interruption?
- did the workbench reduce repeated explanation?
- would people voluntarily return without attendance pressure?
